PRoRounds — Assisted Coaching

Screen mockups for the post-MVP assisted coaching feature, per the five locked decisions in docs/COACHING_UX_BRIEF.md. Built on the same tokens as the core screens — the ring stays hero, the coach sits under it. Section B puts the brief's four open questions side by side to be settled.

A. The coached flow — seven screens

Catalog → editor → ready → coaching sheet → running → minimal → settings. Every coaching control edits one of exactly two stored values: a Configuration field (whether this workout is coached, and at what level) or a SettingsStore preference (call style, ticker visibility).

B. The four open questions

Each is a fork the brief deliberately left to this stage. All four were settled on 2026-09-07 — the chosen option carries a red marker, and section A is drawn to match. The rejected options are kept here with their trade-offs so the reasoning survives into the OpenSpec change.

Q1 — How the Coach ▾ chip sits against the transport controls

A — Pill above the transport chosen Reads as a setting for the run you are about to start, right where your thumb already is. Costs ~50pt of vertical space, and it disappears cleanly the moment the workout starts.
B — In the header row Costs no vertical space and never competes with the play button. But it lands far from the thumb, and at 12.5pt the level text starts to strain at arm's length.
Coach off
Beginner
C — Inline segmented toggle One tap to switch the coach on or off with no sheet at all. It breaks down as soon as level 2 exists — three-plus segments, and no room for call style — so it buys speed now at the cost of a rework later.

Q3 — Ticker type scale and fade timing at arm's length

All three drawn at true 340pt phone width. The call arrives with the audio, holds, then crossfades as the next clip fires — cadence runs 4–10s depending on the arc segment, so the hold is what dominates.
